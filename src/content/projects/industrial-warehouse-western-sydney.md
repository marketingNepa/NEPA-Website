A large industrial warehouse and distribution facility in Western Sydney, where fire protection, hydraulic and electrical engineering had to suit high-bay storage and heavy power demand.

## Scope

- **Fire protection** — high-hazard sprinkler design, hydrant coverage and a dedicated fire pump set sized to the storage classification.
- **Hydraulic** — trade waste, stormwater and on-site detention for a large roof catchment.
- **Electrical** — mains distribution and switchboards sized for warehouse plant and future expansion.

## Outcome

Smart hydrant and pump design reduced the required infrastructure, while electrical capacity was planned with headroom for the facility to grow.
