NEPA Engineering delivered the complete building-services design for a mid-rise residential apartment development in Hurstville, coordinating all four disciplines through a single accountable team.

## Scope

- **Fire protection** — sprinkler and hydrant systems, detection and alarm, and certifiable fire documentation.
- **Hydraulic** — water, gas, sanitary drainage and stormwater, with a rainwater-reuse strategy sized to the development's irrigation and amenity demand.
- **Mechanical** — apartment ventilation, car-park ventilation and smoke management.
- **Electrical** — mains distribution, metering, common-area and emergency lighting.

## Outcome

By resolving service coordination on the drawing board, the project avoided clashes in the ceiling and riser zones and moved through certification without rework — keeping the construction program on track.
