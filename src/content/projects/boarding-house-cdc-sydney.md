A new boarding house in Southern Sydney approved under the Complying Development Certificate (CDC) pathway. Boarding houses have sleeping occupants, so fire safety design is critical — and NEPA delivered the coordinated services documentation the certifier required.

## Scope

- **Fire protection** — sprinkler and detection design for sleeping-occupant risk, emergency lighting, exit signage and fire block plans.
- **Hydraulic** — water, gas and drainage designed for the room configuration.
- **Mechanical** — room and common-area ventilation.
- **Electrical** — distribution, lighting and emergency lighting.

## Outcome

Complete fire safety documentation — fire services drawings, fire safety schedule and compliance review — was prepared to the NCC and Australian Standards, supporting a smooth CDC approval.
