A multi-floor commercial office fit-out in the Sydney CBD, where NEPA Engineering provided the mechanical, electrical and fire-services design to suit the new tenancy layout.

## Scope

- **Mechanical** — reconfigured air-conditioning and air distribution to the new floor plan, with controls tuned for energy efficiency.
- **Electrical** — distribution boards, lighting and emergency lighting, and data and communications infrastructure.
- **Fire protection** — sprinkler and detection modifications coordinated with the base-building systems.

## Outcome

Designs were documented to the National Construction Code and coordinated with the base-building services, allowing the fit-out to be certified and handed over on schedule.
